Under **Dashboard → Community** you find the settings that apply to the whole
community rather than to a single piece of content. This page covers five of
them: **Time zone**, **Navigation**, **Search listing**, **Redirects** and
**Export**.

Two more tabs have their own chapters: **Members** is described under
[Members & roles](/anleitung/mitglieder-und-rollen), **Plan** under
[Billing](/anleitung/abrechnung).

::note
Navigation, search listing and redirects belong to design and are therefore
open to **admins and owners** — the same right as design and branding. The
export is reserved for the **owner** alone.
::

## Your community’s time zone

On the **General** tab you pick your community’s home time zone. It answers a
question nobody thinks to ask — and one that can shift an event by hours:
**whose time does a typed clock time mean?**

- **Without a zone set**, creating an event uses the time zone of whatever
  device is doing the typing. If someone on holiday in Hawaii schedules the
  Hamburg meetup for “19:00”, that means 19:00 Hawaii time — in Hamburg the
  event would sit at 7 in the morning.
- **With a zone set**, typed times always mean the community’s time, no matter
  where a team member happens to be. Recurring events also stay on the same
  wall clock across daylight-saving changes: the weekly 20:00 event stays at
  20:00.

Everyone still sees the event in their own time — the one from their
[account time zone](/anleitung/dein-konto#time-zone). In short: **planned in
the community’s zone, read in yours.**

### An event somewhere else

If an in-person event recognisably takes place in **another country**, the
event form suggests the matching time zone as soon as you type the address:
*“The address points to Japan. Plan the time in Asia/Tokyo?”* One click applies
the zone **to this one event** — the time you typed stays as it is and now
means local time. A second click takes you back to the community’s zone.

::note
The suggestion works without any external service — no address ever leaves
your community. In return it is deliberately cautious: for countries with
several time zones (USA, Canada, Australia …) and for ambiguous addresses it
stays silent. Without a suggestion, typed times keep meaning the community’s
zone — convert the local time yourself in that case.
::

## Putting the menu together

The **Navigation** tab decides what appears in your community's menu, in which
order and under which name.

| Action | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Hide** | The entry disappears from the menu. The page itself stays reachable. |
| **Reorder** | Up, down — the menu follows the order of the list. |
| **Rename** | Your own name instead of the built-in one. An empty field means: keep the built-in name. |
| **Add link** | A page of this community, or an external address. |

Your own links come in two kinds: **a page of this community** (from the list
of your published [pages](/anleitung/produkte/seiten)) or **an external
address**. External addresses have to start with `https://` and open in a new
tab.

To the right there is a **preview** in the order visitors will see. Entries
beyond the fifth move into a "More" menu — a menu that grows off the screen
helps nobody.

::note
**One name covers both languages.** What you type here appears in German and in
English alike — Pukalani does not translate it. If you run a bilingual
community and want the entry named correctly in both, leave the field empty:
the built-in name applies then, and that one is translated.
::

Three things that deliberately do not work:

- **Renaming yes, redirecting no.** A built-in entry always keeps its target.
  If "Posts" suddenly led somewhere else, the menu would be a trap — for your
  own target, add your own link.
- **What your plan does not include stays hidden** — even if you show it. Your
  choice is still saved and takes effect as soon as the product is available.
- **No rebuilding of addresses.** The menu changes the label, not the path.
  What old paths are for is covered under redirects below.

**Reset to the default menu** restores the shipped state in one step.

## Search listing

The **Search listing** tab decides how your community shows up in search
engines — and whether it should show up at all.

### The description

The field **Description of your home page** is the text that appears below the
title in a search result. One or two sentences: what is this about?

- **Up to 160 characters are recommended**; search engines usually cut off
  beyond that. The counter under the field keeps track, and the hard maximum is
  320.
- **Empty** does not mean "nothing": the first paragraph of your home page is
  used instead — exactly as before.
- **Empty and no home page**: the search result shows no description at all.

### Keeping out of search engines

A switch asks search engines not to list the community in their results. Two
things to know about it:

::warning
**This does not make the community private.** Anyone with the link still gets
in. If that is what you want, the **members-only** setting under *General* is
the right place. (For courses, the overview and preview stay visible to
everyone even then; only members see the lessons.)
::

::note
**It takes effect the next time a search engine visits.** Pages that are
already listed do not disappear right away — it can take days or weeks until a
search engine comes back. The same is true in reverse: getting back into the
index also waits for the next visit.
::

### Preview

Below that you see both side by side:

1. **In search** — how your listing looks in a search result.
2. **When a link is shared** — the preview card that appears in messengers and
   social networks. It is generated **automatically** from your community's
   colour and name; uploading your own image is deliberately not offered here.

## Redirects

When you rename or move a page, old links break. Under **Redirects** you say
where they should lead instead.

A redirect consists of two entries:

| Field | What goes in |
|---|---|
| **Old address** | A path on your community, starting with `/` — for example `/about` |
| **New target** | Either a path on this site (`/team`) or a full `https://` address |

What you can expect:

- **Exactly this one address.** There are no wildcards — every old address gets
  its own row. Up to **100** redirects are possible.
- **The German version is caught as well.** You enter the path once; the
  version under `/de/…` is covered too.
- **For a target on this site the language is preserved** — whoever was
  browsing in German lands on the German version.
- **Query parameters travel along.** Whatever follows the `?` is not lost.
- **A target on this site counts as a permanent move**, a target on another
  site as a temporary one. That is why an internal move takes the old
  address's search standing with it, and an external one does not.

Two limits that Pukalani enforces when saving:

- **No chains and no loops.** A target must not itself appear as an old address
  in the list — otherwise visitors are redirected twice in a row, or it goes
  round in circles.
- **Areas the platform needs for itself** cannot be redirected: sign-in,
  administration, home page. Product paths such as `/feed`, on the other hand,
  can.

## Export: taking everything with you

The **Export** tab downloads what is in this community as **one single JSON
file**. Exporting changes nothing in your community, and it works even while
the community is read-only because of an open payment: your content is never
held hostage.

| What's included | What's not included |
|---|---|
| Posts | The member list — ordinary members appear as a count only |
| Comments | Email addresses |
| Pages | Private messages |
| Events | Moderation reports |
| The structure of your courses | |
| Your team with name and role | |

::note
**Why the member list is missing.** Content carries the display name of its
author — an archive without authors would be worthless. But your members'
contact details are their data, not yours: addresses, open invitations and
account identifiers therefore stay out. The bundle states what it leaves out,
so you can tell an empty community from an incomplete export.
::

The export is limited to **two downloads per minute**. Anyone clicking faster
reads a note and tries again a moment later.
