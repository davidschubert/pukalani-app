About us

We are a craft coffee roastery in Upcountry Maui.

It started with one drum roaster in a shed and a lot of ruined batches. Twelve years later the shed is a roastery and the ruined batches are rare.

Why choose us?

Why choose us? Because we buy direct from the farm and pass the story on. Every bag lists the farm, the elevation and the week it was roasted.

Our values

Craft, community and sustainability guide everything we do.
